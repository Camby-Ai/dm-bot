DM Bot · Setup Guide

00 / Comment a word, get the link

Your own DM bot.
No per person bill.

Someone comments a word on your reel, or DMs it to you, and your link lands in their DMs. Most people rent that feature and pay for every person it talks to. This is the one we built for ourselves, and you can download it.

It runs in your own Cloudflare and Meta accounts. The code is free under the MIT license. What follows is what it does, what you need, the six Meta steps, how to test it without messaging anyone by mistake, and the rules Instagram enforces.

01 / What it does

One word in.
One link out.

  • They ask. Someone comments your keyword on a reel, or DMs it to your account.
  • It asks one question. A reply goes under the comment and one question goes to their DMs, with tap answers if you want them.
  • They answer. Typed or tapped. The answer is saved next to their name.
  • It sends the link. As a button, and clicks are counted.
  • Nobody is held hostage. If they never answer, the link goes out anyway after 60 minutes. You can change the wait.
  • Then it stops. One run per person per keyword. After the link, the conversation is yours to have by hand.

It has a small admin page behind a password: your keywords, the messages, a log of everything sent, the people who asked and what they answered.

03 / Get it running

Four steps.
Nothing is sent yet.

01

Download the code

Download the ZIP and unzip it, or clone the repository. Open a terminal in that folder.

02

Set your details

Open wrangler.jsonc and set your Instagram handle, your business name and a contact email. The last two appear on the privacy page Meta asks for.

03

Deploy it in dry run

Dry run is on by default. It logs what it would send and sends nothing.

Run in the folder
npm install
npx wrangler login
npx wrangler secret put ADMIN_PASSWORD
npx wrangler secret put VERIFY_TOKEN
npx wrangler deploy

The admin password is yours to pick. The verify token is any random string. You will paste it into Meta in step 4 of the next section. The last command prints your bot’s address.

04

Open the admin page

Go to your bot’s address followed by /admin and sign in. The Setup tab lists the three addresses Meta needs: the webhook, the privacy page and the data deletion page.

04 / The Meta side

Six steps.
Most of the hour is here.

01

Make a free Meta app

At developers.facebook.com, create an app and pick the use case Manage messaging & content on Instagram.

02

Add your Instagram as a tester

In the app: App roles, Add people, Instagram Tester, and type your handle. Then on instagram.com: Settings, Apps and websites, Tester invites, Accept.

03

Connect your account and generate a token

Under API setup with Instagram login, add your account and generate a token. Copy the account ID shown beside it into IG_USER_ID in wrangler.jsonc, then store the token and the app secret.

Run in the folder
npx wrangler secret put IG_ACCESS_TOKEN
npx wrangler secret put IG_APP_SECRET
npx wrangler deploy

The app secret is the Instagram app secret on the Instagram login page, not the Meta app secret. The bot uses it to check that each message really came from Meta.

04

Point a webhook at your bot

Callback URL: your bot’s address followed by /webhook. Verify token: the string you stored earlier. Then turn on comments, messages and messaging_postbacks.

05

Publish the app

Paste in the privacy and data deletion addresses from the Setup tab. Instagram sends nothing to an unpublished app.

06

On your phone, allow access to messages

Instagram, Settings, Messages and story replies, Message controls, Connected tools, Allow access to messages.

Meta moves its menus

These are the names we clicked in November 2026. If a screen looks different, paste what you see into your AI with the step you are on and let it find the new name.

05 / Go live safely

Test it before a stranger does

  • Make one automation. A keyword, the question, the link. Press Go live. In dry run that still sends nothing.
  • Simulate it. The Setup tab has a Simulate button. Read the Log tab and check every message it would have sent.
  • Turn dry run off. Set DRY_RUN to "false" in wrangler.jsonc and deploy again.
  • Test from the second account. One comment and one DM. Your own account’s comments are ignored on purpose.
  • Then post the reel. Not before. A broken keyword on a live reel is a promise you are not keeping.
Pick the keyword with care

A keyword that is a common word will fire on ordinary messages. Match it only when the whole message is that word, or choose a word people do not use by accident.

06 / Be honest about it

What Instagram will not let it do

The rules and the limits
RuleWhat it meansWhat the bot does
One reply per comment You get one private reply to a comment, within 7 days of it It uses that reply for the question
The 24 hour window After that you can only message someone within 24 hours of their last message or tap It stops and leaves the rest to you
The 60 minute follow-up Someone who commented and never answered may be outside what Instagram allows It tries, logs the error and marks them expired. We have not tested this path on comments
Tap answers Instagram may refuse buttons on the first reply to a comment It sends the question again as plain text. Untested
Two kinds of token An Instagram login token, or a system-user token from Meta Business settings It handles both. Ours runs on the second. We have not run the first one live
Your own comments The bot never reacts to your own account So test from a second account

Check these against Meta’s documentation before you rely on them. Meta changes its rules, and a bot that breaks them can lose its access.

This is a tool for sending people something they asked for. It is not a way to message people who did not ask.
